## Introduction

Welcome to the Library Management System! This manual will guide you through using the system as a library member.

### What You Can Do
- Search for books in the library catalog
- View your borrowing history
- Check due dates for borrowed books
- View fines and payments
- Update your profile information

---

## Getting Started

### Logging In

1. Launch the Library Management System application
2. Enter your email address
3. Enter your password
4. Select "Member" from the "Login As" dropdown
5. Click "Sign In"

**Forgot Password?**
- Click "Forgot Password?" link below the Sign In button
- Enter your email address
- Follow the instructions in the email to reset your password

---

## Member Dashboard

After logging in, you'll see the Member Dashboard with:

### Overview Cards
- **Books Borrowed**: Number of books currently borrowed
- **Overdue Books**: Number of books past their due date
- **Pending Fines**: Total amount of unpaid fines

### Navigation Menu
- **Dashboard**: Overview of your account
- **Search**: Search for books in the catalog
- **My Books**: View your borrowed books
- **Fines**: View and pay fines
- **Profile**: Update your information

---

## Searching for Books

### Basic Search

1. Click "Search" in the navigation menu
2. Enter search terms in the search box (title, author, ISBN, or subject)
3. Click the "🔍 Search" button or press Enter

### Search Results

Search results show:
- **Book Title**
- **Author**
- **ISBN**
- **Category**
- **Publication Year**
- **Availability Status** (Available/Unavailable)
- **Number of Available Copies**

### Filtering Results

- Use the category filter dropdown to narrow results
- Click on category chips to filter by topic

## Viewing Your Account

### My Books

View all books you currently have borrowed:
- Book title and author
- Borrow date
- Due date
- Days remaining or overdue status

### Fines

View your fines:
- Fine amount
- Reason for fine
- Status (Unpaid/Paid/Waived)
- Payment date (if paid)

### Profile

Update your personal information:
- Email address
- Phone number
- Address
- Emergency contact information

## Troubleshooting

### Can't Log In?

1. **Check your credentials**: Make sure email and password are correct
2. **Forgot password**: Use the "Forgot Password?" link
3. **Account locked**: Contact library administrator
4. **Wrong role**: Make sure "Member" is selected in "Login As"

### Search Not Working?

1. Check your internet connection
2. Try different search terms
3. Clear the search and try again
4. Contact library staff if problem persists

### Can't See My Books?

1. Make sure you're logged in as "Member"
2. Check the "My Books" section in the dashboard
3. Contact library staff if books are missing

---

## Tips

- **Search Tips**: Use specific terms for better results (e.g., "Harry Potter" instead of "harry")
- **Due Dates**: Check due dates regularly to avoid fines
- **Renewals**: Contact library staff to renew books before due date
- **Fines**: Pay fines promptly to avoid account restrictions
